Artificial Intelligence is transforming industries across healthcare, finance, and education.

Machine learning is a subset of AI that allows systems to learn from data without explicit programming.

Deep learning uses neural networks with multiple hidden layers to recognize complex patterns.

Natural Language Processing helps computers understand and generate human language.

Semantic search retrieves information based on meaning instead of exact keyword matching.

Vector embeddings convert text into numerical representations that capture semantic meaning.

FAISS is a vector database library used for efficient similarity search.

Computer vision enables machines to analyze and understand images and videos.

Large Language Models are trained on massive datasets to generate human-like responses.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines document retrieval with text generation.

Cloud computing provides scalable infrastructure for AI applications.

Cybersecurity protects systems and networks from digital attacks.

Data science involves collecting, analyzing, and interpreting large datasets.

Python is one of the most popular programming languages for artificial intelligence development.

Reinforcement learning trains agents through rewards and penalties.
